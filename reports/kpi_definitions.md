## 1. Business Context & Golden Revenue Rule

An e-commerce business relies on transactional records to gauge financial health, logistics performance, and customer retention. Raw transaction logs cannot be used blindly for topline revenue reporting because orders that are cancelled before shipment or returned after receipt do not generate retained cash flow.

### The Golden Net Revenue Rule
> **Net Revenue** counts orders with `Order_Status` in **`['Delivered', 'Shipped']`** only.  
> Any order marked as **`'Cancelled'`** or **`'Returned'`** is strictly excluded from Net Revenue and Net Order metrics.

## 3. Comprehensive KPI Definitions (13 Core KPIs)

### KPI 1: Net Revenue
* **Formula:** $\sum \text{Total\_Amount} \quad \text{where } \text{Order\_Status} \in \{\text{'Delivered'}, \text{'Shipped'}\}$
* **Format:** Indian Rupee formatting (e.g., `₹2.95 Cr` or `₹42.50 Lakh`).
* **Why it matters:** Establishes the real top-line financial volume generated by fulfilled demand.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Net Revenue represents the actual realized sales by summing the final invoice amount of all successfully delivered and shipped orders while filtering out returns and cancellations."*

### KPI 2: Total Orders
* **Formula:** $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT } \text{Order\_ID})$
* **Format:** Integer with thousands separator (e.g., `9,900`).
* **Why it matters:** Measures aggregate commercial transaction volume and order demand.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Total Orders reflects gross customer purchase attempts across the entire catalog."*

### KPI 3: Unique Customers
* **Formula:** $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT } \text{Customer\_ID})$
* **Format:** Integer (e.g., `2,412`).
* **Why it matters:** Indicates total customer acquisition footprint across the platform.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Unique Customers quantifies the active distinct buyer base during the selected period."*

### KPI 4: Average Order Value (AOV)
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{Net Revenue}}{\text{Net Orders}} = \frac{\sum_{\text{Delivered, Shipped}} \text{Total\_Amount}}{\text{COUNT}_{\text{Delivered, Shipped}}(\text{Order\_ID})}$
* **Format:** Currency in INR (e.g., `₹3,150`).
* **Why it matters:** Tracks basket size; higher AOV implies more effective bundling or cross-selling.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Average Order Value calculates the average monetary spend per fulfilled transaction."*

### KPI 5: Revenue per Customer
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{Net Revenue}}{\text{Unique Customers}}$
* **Format:** Currency in INR (e.g., `₹12,420`).
* **Why it matters:** Evaluates customer monetization and lifetime value potential.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Revenue per Customer evaluates how much net commercial value each individual shopper contributes on average."*

### KPI 6: Repeat Purchase Rate
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{COUNT}(\text{Customer\_ID with } \ge 2 \text{ total orders})}{\text{Unique Customers}} \times 100\%$
* **Format:** Percentage (e.g., `64.2%`).
* **Why it matters:** Core metric for customer retention, loyalty, and brand affinity.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Repeat Purchase Rate measures customer loyalty by computing the proportion of buyers who returned to place two or more orders."*

### KPI 7: Cancellation Rate
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{COUNT}(\text{Order\_Status} = \text{'Cancelled'})}{\text{Total Orders}} \times 100\%$
* **Format:** Percentage (e.g., `6.5%`).
* **Why it matters:** Identifies friction in checkout, impulsive purchasing, or delivery delays before dispatch.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Cancellation Rate captures pre-dispatch drop-offs, indicating operational friction or customer second thoughts."*

### KPI 8: Return Rate
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{COUNT}(\text{Order\_Status} = \text{'Returned'})}{\text{COUNT}(\text{Order\_Status} \in \{\text{'Delivered'}, \text{'Returned'}\})} \times 100\%$
* **Format:** Percentage (e.g., `7.8%`).
* **Why it matters:** Reflects product catalog accuracy, quality issues, or sizing mismatches after delivery.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Return Rate isolates post-delivery product dissatisfaction as a fraction of all dispatched orders that reached customers."*

### KPI 9: Average Discount %
* **Formula:** $\frac{1}{N} \sum \text{Discount\_Pct}$
* **Format:** Percentage (e.g., `14.8%`).
* **Why it matters:** Gauges promotional intensity and profit margin erosion across merchandise.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Average Discount Percentage shows the overall promotional price reduction offered to buyers across orders."*

### KPI 10: Average Delivery Days
* **Formula:** $\text{MEAN}(\text{Delivery\_Days}) \quad \text{where } \text{Order\_Status} \in \{\text{'Delivered'}, \text{'Returned'}\} \text{ and } \text{Delivery\_Days is not null}$
* **Format:** Decimal days (e.g., `4.2 days`).
* **Why it matters:** Direct operational KPI for supply chain velocity and 3PL courier performance.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Average Delivery Days measures the logistics fulfillment turnaround time from order placement to physical doorstep arrival."*

### KPI 11: Average Rating
* **Formula:** $\text{MEAN}(\text{Customer\_Rating}) \quad \text{where } \text{Customer\_Rating is not null}$
* **Format:** Star rating score (e.g., `4.18 / 5.0`).
* **Why it matters:** Primary voice-of-customer indicator reflecting overall satisfaction.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Average Rating summarizes subjective customer satisfaction based on submitted post-purchase star reviews."*

### KPI 12: Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{Net Revenue}_{\text{Current Month}} - \text{Net Revenue}_{\text{Prior Month}}}{\text{Net Revenue}_{\text{Prior Month}}} \times 100\%$
* **Format:** Signed percentage (e.g., `+18.4%` or `-3.2%`).
* **Why it matters:** Tracks business momentum, seasonality, and demand expansion over time.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"MoM Growth tracks monthly revenue trajectory to highlight accelerating sales periods like festive seasons."*

### KPI 13: Top Segment Share
* **Formula:** $\frac{\max_{s \in \text{Segments}}(\text{Net Revenue}_s)}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100\%$
* **Format:** Percentage with segment name (e.g., `Loyal: 38.5%`).
* **Why it matters:** Highlights customer concentration risk and identifies which segment dominates sales.
* **One-sentence Viva pitch:** *"Top Segment Share reveals revenue concentration by showing what percentage of sales is driven by our single most valuable customer segment."*

## 4. Edge Case & Zero-State Protocols

To ensure 100% dashboard reliability:
1. **Division by Zero:** When denominator is zero (e.g., 0 delivered orders in filtered selection), functions return `0.0` or `'N/A'` rather than crashing.
2. **Missing Ratings / Delivery Days:** Strictly ignore null values from averages without treating nulls as zero.
3. **Empty Filter Results:** Display an informative warning: *"No transactions match the selected filter criteria. Please adjust or reset the filters."*
